In an era where human-made content is struggling against the machine, authentic
pockets of the internet are much rarer. I find myself compelled to document
somewhere the interesting finds I stumble upon as I browse. And so here it is.

## Personal sites

- [burntsushi.net](https://burntsushi.net/) by Andrew Gallant, the creator of
  [ripgrep](https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep), among many other awesome
  software.
- [muan.co](https://muan.co/) by Mu-An Chiou, one of the orignal inspirations to
  this site.
- [agwa.name](https://agwa.name/blog/) by Andrew Ayer, the creator of
  [`git-crypt`](https://agwa.name/projects/git-crypt/) and founder of
  [SSLMate](https://sslmate.com/).
- [pavelfatin.com](https://pavelfatin.com/) by Pavel Fatin, a programmer who
  seems obsessed satisfying interactions with the computer (cf [Typing with
  pleasure](https://pavelfatin.com/typing-with-pleasure/) and [Scrolling with
  pleasure](https://pavelfatin.com/scrolling-with-pleasure/)).
- [terrytao.wordpress.com](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/) by [Terrence
  Tao](https://wikipedia.org/wiki/Terence_Tao).
- [andrewkelley.me](https://andrewkelley.me/) by Andrew Kelley, the creator of
  [Zig](https://ziglang.org/).
- [typesanitizer.com](https://typesanitizer.com/blog/zig-generics.html) by Varun
  Gandhi.
- [dbushell.com](https://dbushell.com/) by David Bushell, who's [saying no to
  AI](https://dbushell.com/ai/).
- [art.fberriman.com](https://art.fberriman.com/) by Frances Berriman, an
  artist.
- [andersnoren.se](https://andersnoren.se/) by Anders Norén, a freelance
  designer & developer living in the Swedish mountains.

## Concepts that exist

- [Hyrum's Law](https://www.hyrumslaw.com/): "With a sufficient number of users
  of an API, it does not matter what you promise in the contract: all observable
  behaviors of your system will be depended on by somebody."

  solution rather than your actual problem.
